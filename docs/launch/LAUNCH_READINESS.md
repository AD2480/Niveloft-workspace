## What the statuses mean here

- **PASS (code)**: Verified at this commit by a check that ran in the sandbox, named in the evidence. It still needs an owner's name and a re-run on the intended release before sign-off.
- **FAIL**: The control is missing or known not to meet the check; the next step says how to fix it or what to switch off.
- **UNVERIFIED**: No adequate evidence yet: implemented but not run here, or it depends on the deployed environment, an account setting, a provider or a person.
- **N/A**: The capability or risk is absent from this release, with the reason; reopen if that changes.
