## dnd-kit — MIT License

- **What:** the collision-detection strategy for multiple sortable containers, used in `frontend/src/features/tasks/KanbanBoard.tsx` (`collisionDetection`). It prefers the droppable under the pointer, falls back to rectangle intersection, resolves a column to its closest card, and caches the last target. It was adapted from the "MultipleContainers" story in `stories/2 - Presets/Sortable/MultipleContainers.tsx`.
- **Source:** https://github.com/clauderic/dnd-kit, tag `@dnd-kit/sortable@10.0.0` (commit `2d86292`).
- **Changes:**
  - Rewritten for the app's `col:<STATUS>` column ids.
  - Removed the "trash" and container-sorting branches.
  - Combined with the app's workflow drop rules.

## The forecast kit's Python libraries (installed separately, not included)

`ml/` uses pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn and joblib (all BSD-3-Clause), and optionally XGBoost and AutoGluon (both Apache-2.0). They're installed with `pip` from `ml/requirements.txt` and `ml/requirements-extra.txt`; no copy of them is in this repository. No dataset is included either: the public datasets the kit can read are downloaded by whoever runs it, under their own licences (see `ml/datasets.json`).
